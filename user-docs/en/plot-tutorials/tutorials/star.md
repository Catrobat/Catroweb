When the plot starts, every movement is drawn as if with a pen. These are the lines the plotter cuts along later.

This star has 6 points. A point is a straight line ("Move 100 steps"), a turn and another straight line that finishes the shape of the point. Before the next point, you have to turn in the opposite direction, so that the next point comes out right.

***Note**: A full circle is 360°. For each point we turn 100° to the right and 40° to the left, which is 60° to the right in total.*

*For all six points we get a turn of 360° to the right.*

**Go further**: Try different combinations of turns and change the number of points.
