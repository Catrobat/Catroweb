Catrobat is a free and open-source **visual programming language** designed to help anyone – from beginners to advanced users – create their own games, animations, and interactive stories directly on their mobile devices. Built around a simple, **block-based** coding interface, Catrobat uses code “bricks” that you can easily drag and drop to build your projects step by step – no prior experience required!

Through Catrobat’s flagship app, **Pocket Code**, you can create digital projects that now go beyond the screen: in addition to traditional coding projects, Catrobat supports makerspace creativity by allowing you to create **DST files for embroidery machines** and **SVG files for cutting plotters**. This means you can bring your digital designs to life in fabric and other physical materials!

Whether you’re a student, hobbyist, or educator, Catrobat offers a fun and hands-on way to explore coding, problem-solving, and digital fabrication.
