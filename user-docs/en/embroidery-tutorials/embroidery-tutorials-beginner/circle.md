In this tutorial, you will learn how to embroider a circle (76.2 x 76.2mm).

![image (6)](../../../media/embroidery-tutorials/image-6.png)

You should already know how to create a new object and insert bricks there. Feel free to check out the tutorials in the [Starter series](../../game-tutorials/starter-tutorials/index.md) if you still need help. Additionally, the embroidery bricks must already be activated. You can find out how to do this in the the **[Activate Embroidery-Bricks](activate-embroidery-bricks.md)**-tutorial.

For more information or design suggestions check out the other tutorials.

*Here you can download the tutorial in PDF-format*:

[Circle](../../../media/embroidery-tutorials/Circle.pdf)

### 1. Create a new Embroidery-Object (see [Create Embroidery-Objects](create-embroidery-objects.md)).

### 2. Now start and insert the bricks step by step to your script.

![Circle](../../../media/embroidery-tutorials/Circle.png)

### 3. Tap play to start your design!

![Circle (1)](../../../media/embroidery-tutorials/Circle-1.png)

You must specify the degree of rotation, as 360 ÷ 60 here.

360 always remains the same for a circle! You must therefore set the repetitions appropriately, such as 60 here. **Try out other combinations!** e.g.: 30 and 360 ÷ 30 or 120 and 360 ÷ 120.
