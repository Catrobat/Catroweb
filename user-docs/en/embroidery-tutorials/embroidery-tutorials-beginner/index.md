Do you want to be creative and at the same time learn coding in a playful way? Do you also like to create things that you can use, wear, and admire right afterward?

With Embroidery Designer you can, without any prior experience, program an embroidery machine that will automatically embroider your design on a T-shirt, bag, pants, smartphone case, or even your shoes. Stitching is possible on everything that is made of fabric. Give free rein to your imagination and creativity! You can download and edit designs from others from our Catrobat Community. Of course, you can also share your projects with your friends and the whole world!
