In this tutorial, you will learn how to save and share your finished designs on your mobile device.

For this, you will need a completed embroidery design that you want to export. If you don’t have one yet, feel free to get inspired by our [embroidery tutorials](index.md)!

*Here you can download the tutorial in PDF-format*:

[Export Designs](../../../media/embroidery-tutorials/Export-Designs.pdf)

### 1. Start your project and wait until it is fully unfolded. Otherwise the pattern will be incomplete.

### 2. Tap back and press the share button.

![Export Designs (1)](../../../media/embroidery-tutorials/Export-Designs-1.png)

### 3. Share your embroidery-file via e-mail or save it to your phone.

![Export Designs (2)](../../../media/embroidery-tutorials/Export-Designs-2.png)

The share-function may look slightly different on every phone.

Your embroidery-design will be saved as DST-file.

The embroidery machine knows this file format and completes your design on your fabric.
